How this is built

Figures come from official releases: the National Health Accounts prepared by the National Health Systems Resource Centre, the National Family Health Survey run by IIPS for the Ministry of Health and Family Welfare, and the Registrar General’s Sample Registration System. Each measure links to the release or summary it was taken from.

NFHS-6 results are provisional, and its fact sheet dropped several indicators, including anaemia and infant mortality. Spending shares use the old GDP base so the series stays comparable; on the new base, 2022-23 is 1.48% of GDP.
