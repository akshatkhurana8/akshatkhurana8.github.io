How this is built

All performance figures are NHS England’s official monthly statistics for England, taken from the publications and analyses linked beside each measure. The waiting list includes NHS England’s estimates for trusts that did not submit data, and A&E figures cover all types of department.

Targets for 2026/27 to 2028/29 come from the NHS Medium Term Planning Framework. The 18-week projection is a straight line through the past 12 months. It shows the pace the targets demand, and it is not a forecast.
