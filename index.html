About

A CV can’t tell a life story, so here’s the short version.

I started out in commercial lending at NatWest Group, analysing financial statements and credit risk. I then retrained in social work, specialising in public health at the University of Delhi, and spent the next few years working with communities in Delhi and Mumbai, alongside children, sex workers, patients and frontline health workers.

My MSc at LSE brought those threads together. I care about research that actually informs decisions, and my finance background gives me a strong interest in how health systems are financed, organised and delivered. Unusually for a social scientist, I’m as comfortable with a spreadsheet as with a survey.

Outside work, I’m a big Chelsea fan, and I use photography as a form of expression. I also read and write about psychology, neuroscience and politics, and I’m planning to restart The Critique Mag (opens in a new tab), my content page on Instagram, and to start a podcast.

Selected work

MSc dissertationLSE and LSHTM2026

The effect of income transfers on perinatal depression: a systematic review

Do income transfers reduce depression during pregnancy and after birth? The question sits where social protection meets maternal mental health, and I carried out the review from search strategy to synthesis.

Methods. Searches of MEDLINE, Embase, PsycINFO and EconLit; screening and data extraction following PRISMA 2020; risk-of-bias assessment with RoB 2 and ROBINS-I; synthesis without meta-analysis (SWiM).

Senior Research FellowIndian Council of Medical ResearchJun–Oct 2024

Door-to-door fieldwork for India’s Diet and Biomarker Survey

I led a team of more than ten field investigators and dietitians in Delhi for the Diet and Biomarker Survey in India (DABS-I), a pan-India study run by ICMR’s National Institute of Nutrition. Going door to door across all 54 primary sampling units, we studied Delhi’s population in depth: socio-demographics, diet, nutritional anthropometry and screening for non-communicable diseases.

I designed the community-mapping approach for rural and urban areas, coordinated with local authorities and frontline health workers, trained new staff on survey tools and research ethics, and managed survey workflows, daily reporting and data cleaning for thousands of respondents. The insights on dietary patterns are intended to inform India’s national nutrition and health policy.

Research Assistant (part-time)Safdarjung Hospital, New DelhiAug 2023 – Jan 2024

Mental health and hospital social work

At Safdarjung Hospital, I carried out door-to-door research on the prevalence of depression among women in underserved communities, using the validated PHQ-9 questionnaire, and helped widen access to screening and support.

In hospital and community settings, I’ve counselled patients and managed intake in a community medicine department, organised awareness camps, and delivered counselling on non-communicable diseases and tobacco cessation.

Earlier, during my placement with Udyam Trust, I spent time at AIIMS, Delhi, with special permission from my supervisor, because I wanted a first-hand sense of social work in medical settings.

Community work in Delhi and Mumbai

I’ve worked with communities in Delhi and Mumbai, from children to sex workers, on education, sexual and reproductive health and rights, water, sanitation and hygiene (WASH), women’s rights and child rights. That meant running community interventions such as financial literacy sessions, legal aid camps and cleanliness drives, and organising morchas (rallies).

Across three placements during my MA, I worked closely with partner NGOs on stakeholder engagement and advocacy campaigns, carried out secondary research to inform policy, and handled communications for the organisations, including a case-study documentary featuring community volunteers.
